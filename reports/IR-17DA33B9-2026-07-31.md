## 4. Email Red Flags

- **SPF_FAIL** — Sending IP not authorized by domain's SPF record. Indicates possible spoofed sender.
- **DKIM_FAIL** — DKIM signature verification failed. Email may have been tampered with or forged.
- **DMARC_FAIL** — Email failed DMARC policy check. Domain instructs receivers to treat this as suspicious.
- **REPLY_TO_MISMATCH** — Reply-To domain differs from From domain. Classic phishing technique to redirect replies to attacker.
- **URGENCY_LANGUAGE** — Email contains urgency/threat language designed to pressure recipient into acting without thinking.
